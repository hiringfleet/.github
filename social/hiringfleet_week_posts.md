## Thu 2026-10-08: Checklist: STEM OPT extension essentials

**Visual:** Checklist graphic (4:5) with tick boxes; carousel on LinkedIn/IG

### LinkedIn (09:00 ET)

```
STEM OPT extension: the checklist I wish every F-1 student had 📋

✅ Confirm your degree is on the DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List
✅ Make sure your employer is enrolled in E-Verify
✅ Complete Form I-983 (training plan) with your employer
✅ Get your DSO's recommendation in SEVIS
✅ File Form I-765 up to 90 days before your current OPT EAD expires, and within 60 days of the DSO recommendation
✅ Track unemployment days: 90 on initial OPT, 150 total with STEM OPT
✅ Submit validation reports and I-983 evaluations on time

Miss one step and the whole timeline gets stressful.

## Fri 2026-10-09: Interview prep: answering 'Will you require sponsorship?'

**Visual:** Short screen recording of AI Interview Prep (15-30s), or a quote card of the sample answer

### LinkedIn (09:00 ET)

```
"Will you now or in the future require sponsorship?"

For international students, this one question can decide how an interview goes. Here's how to answer it with confidence 👇

1️⃣ Be honest. Never dodge it.
2️⃣ Lead with what you can do now: "I'm authorized to work on STEM OPT for up to [X] years."
3️⃣ Make it simple for them: "In the future I'd need H-1B sponsorship, and I'm happy to walk through how straightforward that process can be."
4️⃣ Bring it back to value: "In the meantime, I'm focused on [the impact you'll make]."

Short, calm and confident beats a long, nervous explanation every time.
