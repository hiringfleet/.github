# Contributing to HiringFleet

Thanks for your interest in HiringFleet.

Our platform code is currently private while we build toward public launch, so we aren't accepting code contributions yet. That will change — when we open-source parts of the platform, this document will grow the usual sections on setup, style, and pull requests.

In the meantime, here's how you can help:

- **Found a bug on [hiringfleet.com](https://hiringfleet.com)?** Email [support@hiringfleet.com](mailto:support@hiringfleet.com) with what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help.
- **Found a security issue?** Please follow our [security policy](SECURITY.md) instead of a public report.
- **Have sponsorship data corrections or feature ideas?** We read everything sent to [support@hiringfleet.com](mailto:support@hiringfleet.com) — corrections to sponsor listings are especially valuable, since students make real decisions based on them.

All participation in our community is covered by our [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
